Key Takeaways


  1. What are Metaphors?
    Metaphors create symbolic meaning by directly comparing two unrelated things without using “like” or “as.”

  2. Understanding Simile:
    Similes compare two different things using words such as “like” or “as.”

  3. What is Personification?
    Personification gives human traits or emotions to non-human objects or abstract concepts.

  4. Explaining Idioms:
    Idioms are common expressions whose meanings are not literal and often reflect cultural or figurative significance.

  5. What Are Literary Devices?
    Literary devices are tools that are used to express ideas in creative, powerful, and memorable ways.

Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Paraphernalia
Meaning: Equipment, accessories, or miscellaneous items needed for a specific activity.
Example: The hiking paraphernalia included a sturdy backpack, a water bottle, and a map of the trails.

Corral
Meaning: An enclosure used to confine or gather animals, especially livestock.
Example: The ranchers herded the cattle into the corral before the storm began.

Profusely
Meaning: Abundantly or in large amounts; excessively.
Example: The teacher thanked the students profusely for their thoughtful gift.

Splintered
Meaning: Broken into sharp, thin pieces or fragments.
Example: The old wooden bench splintered under the weight of the heavy box.
